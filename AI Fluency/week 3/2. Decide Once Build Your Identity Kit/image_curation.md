## Where I chose a real capture over AI

- **Hero portrait (`hero-photo-real.jpg`)** — This was the biggest fix. I originally had an AI-generated stylized version of myself (GTA VI synthwave style) sitting in the hero. It looked cool, but it's not actually me, and the brief is explicit that anything depicting the person has to be a real photo. Replaced it with a real photo of myself, cropped to sit inside the same palm-tree/sunset backdrop, so the mood is preserved but the person in it is real.
- **About Me** — Already a real photo, kept as-is. No reason to regenerate a "cleaner" version when the authentic one does the job.
- **Working project thumbnails** — The project captures below are real screenshots of actual output, proving the work exists rather than just implying it:
  - **Gesture File Transfer:** `/work-01-gesture-file-transfer.jpg`
  - **Just Divide Game:** `/work-02-just-divide-game.jpg`

  ## What I rejected, and why
 
**Rejected: the AI-generated hero self-portrait.**
It matched my real appearance closely (glasses, jacket, general look) and the synthwave palette tied the whole site together nicely — but it's a fictional rendering of my face, not a photo of me. A recruiter shouldn't have to wonder "is that actually him?" when they land on the hero. Swapped for a real photo; kept the AI background since that's mood/texture, not identity.
 
**Rejected: the Suraksha Setu project image.**
This is illustrated concept art — a cartoon character, the Red Fort, a shield icon — not a screenshot of the actual app. It looks polished, but it's an AI stand-in doing the job a real screenshot should do: proving the work exists and functions. Rather than keep an unverifiable image on the site, I removed this project card entirely until a real screenshot is ready.
 
**Rejected: the Road Accident Detection project image.**
Same problem — a generated diagram of sensors and an LED display mockup, not a capture of a working system. An illustration of what it *would* look like isn't proof of what it *does* look like, so I removed this card from the site as well rather than let concept art stand in for a real result.
 
## Consistency check
The only AI-generated content remaining in the final set is the hero background texture (palm trees, sunset, gradient bars) — one style, used purely as mood/branding, never as a stand-in for a person or for proof of work. Every image showing me is a real photo. Every image proving technical work (where available) is a real screenshot.
